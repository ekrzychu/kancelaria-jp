- Confirm the controller and contact details, processing purposes, whether and how own-service marketing and voluntary data are processed, and the applicable legal bases for website security, logs, analytics and recruitment.
- Confirm the actual hosting, backup, SMTP and mailbox providers, the scope and location of their processing, recipients, contractual terms and any applicable transfer arrangements. No processor agreement or transfer safeguard has been assumed.
- Confirm production request and security log fields, access, retention and purge procedures, including any reverse proxy or provider logs. Verify the cookie inventory on the deployed public form and panel.
- Approve correspondence retention and deletion procedures covering MariaDB, mailbox copies and backups. Approve the analytics activation decision and retention setting separately.
- Confirm the official social profiles and current processing roles, including whether the TikTok channel is active. Confirm how recruitment notices are provided when an offer directs candidates off-site.
- Obtain legal and organizational approval of the final text before removing its draft notice or substituting it for the live policy.
